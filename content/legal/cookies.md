# Cookie Policy

**Version:** 2026-09-14  
**Effective date:** 2026-09-14

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies and similar technologies Klymb AI uses, why, and how you control them. It supplements our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores in your browser. Some are essential - without them you cannot stay signed in. Others are optional and we only set them if you allow us to.

We also use a small amount of **browser storage** (`localStorage` / `sessionStorage`) purely to remember interface state - for example that you already dismissed a tooltip or a promotional dialog. This never leaves your device and is not used to track you.

## 2. Your choice

The first time you visit Klymb AI, we present our cookie banner with three straightforward options:

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Accept all** | Activates strictly necessary sign-in and security cookies, aggregate analytics (Google Analytics 4), referral attribution cookies, and video preview thumbnails loaded from YouTube |
| **Necessary only** | Strictly necessary cookies only. Optional analytics and attribution cookies remain off, and no video thumbnails are fetched |
| **Manage preferences** | Opens our category-level preference center where you can selectively toggle **Analytics & Performance** and **Referral Attribution & Media** |

Strictly necessary cookies cannot be switched off - they are required to authenticate your sign-in and keep your session secure. Choosing **Necessary only** does not reduce your access to any core learning track, submission, or challenge feature; it only means we cannot credit referral invites or measure aggregated platform usage.

Your choice is stored in a cookie (`abtalks_consent`) so we don't prompt you on every page visit. If we make material updates to this Policy, we will prompt you again.

## 3. Cookie categories

### Strictly Necessary Cookies (Always Active)

These cookies are essential for the website to function securely and cannot be switched off:

- **Auth.js session cookie**: Keeps you securely signed in to your account (`httpOnly`, inaccessible to client scripts).
- **`abtalks_consent`**: Remembers your cookie preferences and policy version (~180 days).

### Optional: Analytics & Performance

- **Google Analytics 4**: Measures aggregate, de-identified platform usage (page views, button clicks, enrollments). GA4 runs **only in production** and **only with your explicit analytics consent**. All IP addresses are anonymized and no personal data (email, phone, name) is sent.

### Optional: Referral Attribution & Media

- **`abtalks_ref`**: Remembers referral codes from `?ref=` links so members who invited you receive credit (~7 days).
- **`abtalks_src`**: First-touch attribution from `?s=` links to understand which educational partner brought you here (~30 days).
- **YouTube Media Previews**: Enables preview thumbnails from `youtube-nocookie.com` on program video libraries.

## 4. Third-party scripts and embeds

- **Phone verification (MSG91).** When you use phone/OTP verification, your browser loads a widget script from `verify.msg91.com`. That provider may set its own cookies or storage under its own domain, governed by its own privacy policy. The script loads only at the moment you use phone verification - never on initial page load.
- **Video embeds (YouTube).** All videos use the privacy-extended `youtube-nocookie.com` domain, which does not set advertising cookies.
  - On **program mission pages and the video library**, videos are click-to-load. We show a placeholder and request nothing from Google until you press play. Video preview thumbnails are fetched only if you grant Attribution & Media consent (or choose **Accept all**).
  - On **challenge day pages**, the tutorial player loads together with the page, so Google is contacted when you open that page. We disclose this transparently; if you prefer not to connect to Google, avoid opening tutorial video pages.
- **Google Analytics 4.** We use GA4 solely to measure aggregate usage of the Service. GA4 loads **only** on the production Service and **only** when Analytics consent is enabled. If you choose **Necessary only**, or before you have made a choice, the script is blocked entirely and **no request is made to any Google endpoint** (`googletagmanager.com` or `google-analytics.com`). When consent is partial, Google **Consent Mode v2** denies ad-storage and ad-personalization signals. Data is retained for **14 months**. Changing your preferences from enabled to **Necessary only** unloads the script immediately for future interactions.

## 5. What we do not do

We do **not** sell personal data. We do **not** use Meta Pixel, cross-site advertising networks, third-party data brokers, or behavioural tracking cookies. If our integrations ever change, we will update this Policy and require fresh consent before enabling any new category.

**Do Not Track.** We do not currently alter our cookie practices in response to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Use the choice above (or Manage preferences) to control optional cookies.

## 6. Changing or clearing your choice

- Use the **Manage cookie preferences** button on this page to reopen the preferences modal at any time.
- You can also delete cookies directly in your browser settings. Clearing the Klymb AI cookies will sign you out and cause the chooser to appear again on your next visit.
